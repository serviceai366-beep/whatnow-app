Subscription pricing recommendation

A decision-ready planning model for a sustainable paid plan. No checkout or paid service has been enabled.

Executive Summary

Approved draft launch price: $9.99/month, with tax treatment confirmed before checkout is enabled.

Do not sell literal unlimited usage. A fixed price cannot guarantee profit when one account can submit an unbounded number of large PDFs. Position the plan as high-use personal access with a clear fair-use policy, document ceilings, account-level abuse controls, and a global budget circuit breaker.

Prepare before charging. Measure real input, cached-input, and output tokens for at least 100 successful analyses or 30 days, then confirm the price and safety thresholds from observed usage.

What $9.99 leaves for service costs

At the 17 July 2026 ECB reference rate, $9.99 is approximately €8.71. Using a 21% VAT planning assumption, Stripe's standard EEA card fee of 1.5% + €0.25, and a conservative additional 2% currency-conversion assumption, approximately €6.65 remains before AI, email, database, hosting, support, refunds, and other business costs.

Recommended launch controls

  1. Keep the current Free plan: 3 analyses per rolling 24 hours and 10 per rolling 7 days.
  2. Draft Pro as high-use personal access at $9.99/month; do not enable checkout yet.
  3. Use a transparent safety policy. The approved draft thresholds are 15 analyses per rolling 24 hours and 150 per rolling 30 days, subject to real-cost review.
  4. Keep size and token controls for large documents. A 10 MB file-size ceiling alone does not bound PDF page count or model cost.
  5. Keep authentication, Turnstile, per-account controls, and the service-wide budget stop.
  6. Store only token counts, estimated cost, model, document class, time, and a hashed account key in cost telemetry—never document text.

Further decisions before payments

  • Confirm whether the annual $99.90 option should launch together with the monthly plan.
  • Approve the public fair-use wording and exact safety thresholds.
  • Confirm tax/business readiness, cancellation and refund rules, and the legal seller identity.
  • Choose Stripe Checkout + Billing only after these decisions; payment code is intentionally absent.
