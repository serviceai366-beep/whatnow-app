# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want WhatNow? to be a welcoming project for people with different
backgrounds and experience levels. Contributors, maintainers, and users should
be treated with respect and should feel safe asking questions or reporting
problems.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific in technical feedback.
- Assume good intent while discussing mistakes.
- Protect personal documents, credentials, and other sensitive information.
- Focus on the issue, its evidence, and a practical way forward.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate
publication of secrets or private documents, and disruptive spam are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the
repository's private contact channel. The maintainer may edit, hide, close, or
remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community.
